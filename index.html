Q&A
Why Not Replace Me With an Agent?

An agent runs the tasks you hand it. Someone still has to pick the buying signals, connect the tools and decide which accounts your team talks to. I do that part, and the agents take over the manual work.

Do You Replace My Marketing Team?

No, I work with your team. I give them AI tools that take over the manual work, so a lean team can cover the whole funnel.

What Happens in the Free 15 Minutes?

You walk me through your current funnel, and we pick the first stage to fix. You leave with 1 concrete next step, whether or not we work together.

How Can I Work With You?

Founders bring me in to advise, or to lead the team myself until the new GTM works. You can also rent a single GTM tool for 1 stage of your funnel.

Which Tools Does My Team Need Already?

You don't need any specific tools yet. New tools launch every day, so we might build a custom tool or test a new one. Either way, don't commit to any tool for a year, or even 6 months.
