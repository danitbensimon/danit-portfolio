No BS GTM hacks to book more meetings

Danit Ben Simon · November 21, 2026 · Ads, signals, ABM and outbound

Hiring another SDR used to be the answer to a thin pipeline. In 2026 it mostly buys you a $100K dialer. The teams booking the most meetings flipped the model: 1 marketer with agents does the prospecting, and every move starts with a signal.

Below are 5 of the 32 hacks from my free guide, each with the trigger, the move, the copy and how to measure it. The other 27 are in the PDF.

5 growth rules before any hack

  • Analyze your full pipeline. Campaign results show 1 stage. Once a week, check accounts showing interest, stalled deals, Closed Lost and champions who changed jobs.
  • Run 7 tests a month. Write a 5-line test plan before you start a hack: the accounts, the trigger, the stage it should move, what counts as success and a decision date.
  • Kill or scale. No meeting by your decision date? Stop. Got a meeting? Run it on your next 3 similar accounts.
  • Use data you already have. CRM stages, website visits and LinkedIn job changes. Pull the 3 together once a week.
  • Report meetings, never MQLs. Track meetings booked and deals that moved a stage.

Hack #1: Quote competitor reviews in LinkedIn ads

When: your competitor's customers keep leaving 1-star G2 reviews.

Run LinkedIn ads to those companies that quote the complaint and show your fix.

  1. Pull the competitor's customer list with CompetitorIQ.
  2. Sort their G2 reviews by 1 and 2 stars and pick the 3 complaints that repeat most.
  3. Upload the list as a LinkedIn company list and run 1 ad per complaint.
Copy to use: Still exporting reports by hand in [Competitor]? Here's the 2-click version.

Measure: demo requests from the uploaded companies within 30 days.

Hack #10: Gift your champion's new team free seats

When: your champion moved to a new company.

Send their new team 3 months of free seats in their first week.

  1. Track champions from won deals with a job-change alert in Sales Navigator.
  2. In their first week, send a card with 3 months of free seats for their new team.
  3. In week 3, offer 15 minutes to set it up with them.
Copy to use: Welcome to [new company]. Your new team gets 3 months of [Product] on us.

Measure: a meeting at the new company within 30 days.

Hack #16: Send a private driver

When: your target is attending a conference.

Send a private driver to take them to and from the event, and join the ride.

  1. Check the speaker and attendee list with the Event Attendee Scraper.
  2. Offer the ride 2 weeks before the event.
  3. Ride with them and keep the pitch to 1 question about their goals.
Copy to use: We're both going to [event]. Can I send a car and share the ride?

Measure: a meeting booked from the ride.

Hack #25: Set 100 call attempts a day

When: your SDRs spend their mornings writing emails.

Marketing writes the emails. SDRs make 100 call attempts a day.

  1. Marketing writes and sends the email sequences.
  2. SDRs call accounts with a fresh signal from 9:00 to 12:00 and 14:00 to 16:00.
  3. Track connects and meetings per 100 attempts.
Copy to use: Hi [Name], I'm calling because [signal]. Got 2 minutes?

Measure: meetings per 100 call attempts.

Hack #26: Pull your competitor's customer list

When: you don't know who uses your competitor.

Pull their customer list from 6 signal sources. My WikiLeads build drove a 10x outbound lift.

  1. Enter your competitor's domain at competitorcustomer.com.
  2. Get the customer list with the signal behind each company.
  3. Load it into outbound and LinkedIn ads the same week.
Copy to use: Teams moving off [Competitor] tell us [reason]. Want the 2-minute version?

Measure: meetings from the list within 30 days.
